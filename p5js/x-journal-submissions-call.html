X-Journal Submissions Call

The theme for the issue was liminal, in-between states and things that had not resolved yet.

I built a live text-drawing tool where as you type and each letter appears as a wiggly, connected line that keeps moving after you place it.

The distortion was not planned. It came from pushing the parameters until the letterforms started feeling wrong in the right way, spiked, mutated, familiar enough to read but strange enough to mean something. That felt like liminality: letters that had not fully decided what they were yet.
