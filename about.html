About

Suyash Chitrakar is a designer and creative technologist from Kathmandu, Nepal, based in the American Midwest. He graduated from Knox College in 2025 with a degree in Computer Science and Design, and is currently seeking to pursue graduate studies in design.

He grew up making things. Art classes as a child, then more art classes. At Knox he worked as a designer for X-Journal and Catch Magazine, and designed a book for a faculty member and another book that went on to win an award. He belives that the code is a tool and not the destination.

His work spans graphic design, creative coding, interactive systems, and photography. The common thread is an interest in what happens when a system meets something it didn't account for.

His last name "Chitrakar" comes from Sanskrit, meaning image maker or painter. It was probably always going to end up this way :)

Currently open to freelance work and collaborations.
